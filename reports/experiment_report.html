6. Reproduction Notes & Limitations

  • NoiFi runs on CPU only (no CUDA on macOS) — training is slower but numerically equivalent
  • ZEN requires Linux x86-64 with matching kernel symbols for full forensic pipeline
  • All reported results are from our own runs, not copied from the papers
  • Threshold tuning data and final evaluation data are kept separate
  • Small epoch counts in smoke tests verify pipeline connectivity, not research claims
